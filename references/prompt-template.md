# 生图模板

每张独立调用内置 image_gen，先查看标准图，将其绝对路径放入 referenced_image_paths；编辑同时附目标图。标准图只约束身份，不复制三联排版。

```text
Generate ONE standalone 16:9 horizontal Chinese article illustration.
以参考图为角色唯一依据. Attached reference is final 橘子灯 orange-peel lampshade little traveler. Preserve her compact 1.6 heads tall proportions, softly asymmetric orange hood with ONE turned-back peel rim on HER LEFT (viewer RIGHT frontally), visible cream reverse, two solid dark brown side-part hair shapes, natural moderate full brows, moderate almond eyes, tiny nose and understated expressive mouth, short orange cloak and tiny dark hands/feet. No lashes, blush circles, hoodie, long pants, bag, teardrop sprite body, crescent fringe, robot or anime face.
One green leaf rooted at same hood-top position. Neutral tilts 45 degrees; tired droops; clarity stands up. Only when understanding: narrow warm cream-yellow inner rim plus TWO orange short rays; may lift rim if hands are available. No gradients, glow or shadows. Match emotion to article, no obligatory happiness.
Scene: {原创物理隐喻，角色核心动作、表情、物件}
Core meaning: {一个正文观点}
Exact handwritten Chinese labels: {短标注或无文字}
Natural irregular hand ink doodle, sparse orange/cream/muted blue flat fills, white background, at least 35% whitespace. One scene, one active character unless intentional comic. No triptych, title, PPT, formal flowchart, realistic UI, 3D, vector polish or textured background.
```

修正身份漂移时附标准图和目标图，只改指出的偏差。翻边奶油内侧日常可见，但亮起短光线只在适当情绪下使用。
